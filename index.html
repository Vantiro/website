ERP Bridge

Your ERP data, inside HubSpot.

Sync customers, products, orders, invoices and service tickets from SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, Sage or Cegid. The connection is direct and encrypted, and nothing is stored in between.

  • Live in 30 days
  • Drag-and-drop field mapping
  • 30-day money-back guarantee
Explore ERP Bridge
Loyalty One

A loyalty program that runs from HubSpot.

Set up points, tiers, prizes and contests, and keep every member’s activity in your CRM instead of in a separate tool.

  • Points and tiered memberships
  • QR-code point loading and prize claims
  • Analytics dashboard
Explore Loyalty One
FieldUp

Take HubSpot out into the field.

Create and dispatch work orders from HubSpot tickets. Technicians log jobs, photos and customer signatures on a mobile app, and it all lands back in your CRM.

  • No extra HubSpot seats for technicians
  • Calendar-based dispatching
  • Billing-ready reports with signatures
